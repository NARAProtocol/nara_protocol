# NARA Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-03-22

## Core View

The board is a launch surface. The engine is the real product core.

NARA is the broader protocol:

- fixed supply
- sealed reward reserve
- sealed bond inventory
- weight-based locking
- a fair engine for distributing protocol earnings
- future composability on top of locked positions

If the current board is not the right surface to kick-start the economy, the surface can change. The protocol thesis should not.

## Current Starting Point

- locking is live
- `30,000 NARA` is already locked by treasury and owner
- the reward reserve and bond vault are in place
- the epoch engine is active
- bonds are deployed but intentionally closed
- float is thin enough that early market structure matters

## Phase 1: Locking UX

Immediate priority:

- make the lock flow obvious
- make activation and warmup visible
- reduce friction from buy to lock to claim
- make first positions socially shareable

## Phase 2: Visibility And Analytics

Next priority:

- activation dashboard
- weight leaderboard
- epoch and backlog monitoring
- reward reserve tracking
- public state visibility

## Phase 3: Earnings Surfaces

The key growth path is not adding random features.

The key growth path is building products that can create useful ETH earnings and route them into the engine so lockers share the revenue.

That is how current and future products become one protocol economy instead of disconnected apps.

## Phase 4: Bond Readiness

Bonds should only open when:

- liquidity is stronger
- price discovery is healthier
- operator monitoring is ready
- the market can absorb controlled discounted distribution

## Phase 5: Composability

Long-term upside comes from infrastructure on top of the core protocol:

- lock-position wrapper
- auto-compounding tools
- analytics APIs
- aggregation
- collateral and lending integrations

## Decision Rule

If a surface fails, replace the surface.
Do not abandon the protocol thesis because one onboarding experiment underperforms.
