# Code of conduct

NARA project spaces are for respectful, evidence-based technical collaboration.

Be clear, patient, and constructive. Assume readers have different levels of
crypto knowledge. Challenge claims with evidence rather than attacking people.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, publish private
information, promote scams, manipulate markets, or pressure anyone into a
financial action.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not disclosed publicly before responsible handling.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, misleading, promotional, unsafe, or
off-topic contributions and may restrict participation when needed to protect
the community.

Report conduct concerns to
[security@naraprotocol.pro](mailto:security@naraprotocol.pro). Do not include
unnecessary sensitive personal information.
