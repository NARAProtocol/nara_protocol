## Purpose

This repository provides technical and educational information about
experimental software. It is not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice;
an offer or invitation; a recommendation; a prospectus; a crypto-asset white
paper; or a statement of regulatory approval.

## No offer or promotion

Nothing here asks a person to acquire, hold, sell, stake, lock, or provide
liquidity for NARA. Product status, allocation, and planning figures are factual
descriptions or unexecuted scenarios, not price targets, promised utility,
returns, or commitments.

## Communications standard

Public NARA communications must be factual, current, balanced, understandable to
their intended audience, and must not omit material risks. Benefits must not be
more prominent than related risks. Do not use:

- guaranteed, protected, insured, approved, or risk-free claims;
- price predictions or return projections;
- urgency, scarcity pressure, or “easy money” framing;
- personalized suitability or investment advice;
- “best,” “safest,” “recommended,” or similar asset steering; or
- a “live” label without verified code and onchain evidence.

If a communication becomes a regulated marketing communication, a general
disclaimer does not cure non-compliance. Use the exact notices, approval route,
format, risk warning, and consumer journey required for the intended
jurisdiction only after legal review.
