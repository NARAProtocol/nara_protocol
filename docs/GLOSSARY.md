# Glossary (`GLOSSARY.md`)

- **Base (`8453`)** — The Ethereum Layer-2 network developed by Coinbase where NARA Protocol V5 is deployed.
- **Basis Points (`bps`)** — One hundredth of a percent (`100 bps = 1.00%`, `463 bps = 4.63%`, `10,000 bps = 100.00%`).
- **[CryptoSLIP App](https://cryptoslip-g29s.4everland.app/?v=cs-1794113891779)** — The non-custodial Base payment-link and NARA V5 companion app supporting P2P crypto slips (`USDC`, `ETH`, `cbBTC`, `NARA` starting from `0.001 NARA`), direct V5 commitments (`Commit NARA`, `1.0+ NARA` minimum), `NARAP` NFT trading, and reward harvesting into shareable Slips (see the **[CryptoSLIP Public Overview & Legal FAQ](CRYPTOSLIP_PUBLIC_OVERVIEW_AND_LEGAL_FAQ.md)**).
- **Epoch (`15 Minutes` / `900s`)** — A fixed 15-minute reward window on `NARAEpochEngineV5` (`96 epochs per day`). At the end of each epoch, scheduled `NARA` emissions and incoming `ETH`/`USDC` fees are allocated to active positions.
- **ERC-20Permit (EIP-2612)** — A standard supported by `NARATokenV5` that lets you approve token spending with a free off-chain wallet signature.
- **ERC-3156 Flash Loan** — An instant loan of up to `100,000 NARA` from `NARATokenV5` that must be borrowed and repaid within the exact same blockchain transaction, paying a `0.10%` fee to `NARAEmissionReserveV5`.
- **Gnosis Safe (`2-of-3` Multisig)** — A smart-contract treasury wallet requiring at least 2 out of 3 independent hardware/signer approvals to execute any permitted administrative action.
- **Hook (`NARALiquidityHookV5`)** — Custom smart-contract logic attached to the canonical `NARA / USDC` Uniswap v4 pool that charges a `4.63%` base swap fee (escalating up to `20.00%` during same-block burst trading) and records fees into `NARAFeeVaultV5`.
- **Zero-Delay Fee Controller (`NARATreasuryFeeControllerV5`)** — The controller (`0xB6A436b48222C94fe427ecAfB263DF9cf42646b9`) sealed to `NARAFeeVaultV5` (`0xF1f361F803895BF5cf67eC6C506dbFEf2f6E88Cf`) that allows the `2-of-3` `Treasury Safe` to redeem and route `USDC` and `NARA` swap fees with **`0-second delay`** (`sweepToSafe`, `withdrawAllTo`, `withdrawTo`) to the `Treasury Safe`, `NARATreasuryRangeManagerV5`, `NARAEmissionReserveV5`, or `NARARewardIngressV5`.
- **On-Chain SVG Card (`NARAPositionCardV5` & `NARAPositionRendererV5`)** — The on-chain contracts (`0x12B9F23C24a4A0F29cA66F79183ed4C3f07588F5` and `0x5Fd79Fc807B0e3d1e04A818f304526F8954dbd24`) that render each `NARAP` Position NFT's `1000×1000` brown mineral SVG artwork and JSON metadata directly from live contract state.
- **Position NFT (`NARAP`)** — An individual 1:1 ERC-721 NFT (`NARAPositionNFTV5` `0x8C5F088D9d09dBB90aB6F3aaA049a8FD5D5FfFDA`) minted whenever `1.0+ NARA` is committed into `NARAPrincipalLedgerV5` (never split or fused). Whoever holds the NFT owns the committed principal and unclaimed rewards, and can trade the position on `NARAPositionSaleV5` (`0x04Ac83045d26259D46400Ce6beb9bBd0641df225`) for native `ETH`.
- **Two-Way Range Manager (`NARATreasuryRangeManagerV5`)** — Active range liquidity periphery (`0x221c625d2BC416460C397035C1286C1f4A4D3A7C` bound to the canonical pool, plus legacy custody `0xa72557fcBE56B3eC739dF3aF7b6b893B6Db4D288`) that supplies upside `NARA` sell rungs (`0-second delay` `USDC` settlement to `Treasury Safe`) and downside `USDC` buy-back rungs.
- **Quadratic Time Weight (`V4Quadratic`)** — The mathematical curve (`1.00x` at 1 day up to `4.00x` at 365 days) that gives longer time commitments a larger share of 15-minute epoch rewards.
- **Segregated Principal Custody** — The V5 safety rule where user-committed `NARA` is stored 1-for-1 inside `NARAPrincipalLedgerV5`, completely separated from `NARAEmissionReserveV5` and `NARARewardLedgerV5`.
- **Waiting Period (`7 Days`)** — A mandatory on-chain waiting period (`604,800 seconds`) before scheduled governance actions (such as Range Manager raw `NARA` withdrawals or `NARAFeeVaultV5` automatic `holderFeeBps` split updates) can take effect.
- **USDC** — A widely used USD-referenced stablecoin on Base (`0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913`) paired with `NARA` in the canonical Uniswap v4 pool.
